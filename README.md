# 🛒 Brazilian E-Commerce Sales Analysis
 
An end-to-end data analytics project built on the **Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset** — 53K+ orders across multiple states, cities, and product categories — uncovering the business story hidden inside the numbers.
 
---
 
## 📌 What This Project Is About
 
This isn't just a dashboard. It's an attempt to think like a business analyst — not just visualize data, but **challenge what the data is saying**.
 
The dataset contains real-world e-commerce transactions from across Brazil, spanning different states, cities, customers, and product categories. The goal was to go from raw, messy data → cleaned insights → actionable business decisions.
 
---
 
## 🔍 Key Findings
 
| Metric | Insight |
|---|---|
| 📍 Top State (SP) | 4.38M revenue — next closest is 1.37M |
| 🏙️ São Paulo City | 2.8M alone — more than entire states |
| 📈 Revenue Growth | 121% growth... followed by a sharp drop |
| 💳 Payment Preference | 75% credit card — alternatives barely adopted |
 
**The uncomfortable questions this raised:**
- Why is one region carrying the entire business?
- Is that revenue drop a data issue — or a business crisis?
- How do you scale without dangerous regional dependency?
 
---
 
## 💡 Business Recommendations
 
- **Double down** on top regions, but actively expand into underperforming ones
- **Investigate the revenue drop** before drawing conclusions
- **Push alternative payment adoption** to reduce credit card dependency
- **Treat regional concentration** as a risk, not just a stat
